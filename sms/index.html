HUCKLEBERRY'S DIESEL SERVICES LLC

SMS Messaging Program

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Optional service-related text messages

Huckleberry's Diesel Services LLC offers optional SMS messaging for service-related communications such as scheduling, appointment reminders, job status updates, estimates, parts availability, and customer support.

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services, requesting service, creating an account, or completing a transaction.

How to opt in

To opt in, text START to +1 936-253-6422. By texting START, you expressly consent to receive service-related SMS messages from Huckleberry's Diesel Services LLC at the mobile number you use to opt in.

After opting in, you will receive a confirmation message from Huckleberry's Diesel Services. No SMS consent is collected automatically when you request service or provide a phone number.

Program disclosures

  • Message frequency varies based on your service activity and requests.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact support@docincorporatedllc.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information may be disclosed to service providers only as necessary to operate and deliver the messaging service.

See the Huckleberry's Diesel Services Privacy Policy and Huckleberry's Diesel Services Terms & Conditions for additional information.
