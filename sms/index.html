SMS Messaging Program

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Optional service-related text messages

Huckleberry's Diesel Services LLC offers optional SMS messaging for service-related communications such as scheduling, appointment reminders, job status updates, estimates, parts availability, and customer support.

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services, creating an account, or completing a transaction.

How to opt in

To opt in to this messaging program, text START to +1 936-253-6422. By texting START, you expressly consent to receive service-related SMS messages from Huckleberry's Diesel Services LLC at the mobile number you use to opt in.

You may also opt in through another clearly presented, optional consent method offered directly by Huckleberry's Diesel Services LLC. Any checkbox or similar control used for SMS consent will be optional and unchecked by default.

Program disclosures

  • Message frequency varies based on your service activity and requests.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact support@docincorporatedllc.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information may be disclosed to service providers only as necessary to operate and deliver the messaging service.

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for additional information.
